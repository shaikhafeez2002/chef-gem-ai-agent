# My agent: personal-chef-agent
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps home cooks plan meals, discover recipes, and manage dietary needs with a catalog of recipes and personalized nutrition scaling.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Dietary preferences, allergies, favorite cuisines, cooking skill level
- Tools: Recipe search, ingredient substitution lookup
- Catalog/UI: Recipe cards with prep time, difficulty rating, and ingredient list; meal plan tables
- Image gen: Plated dish image generation for customized recipe suggestions
- Sandbox: Serving size scaling and macro/nutritional calculation

Core rails (everyone): memory, tools, eval, deploy, frontend
My stretch menu (pick later): A2UI recipe cards, image generation, sandbox serving calculator
First eval question: Suggest a quick 20-minute dinner using chicken and broccoli that strictly avoids nuts (user allergy) and displays macros.
